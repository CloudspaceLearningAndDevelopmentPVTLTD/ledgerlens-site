LedgerLens

A privacy-first ledger for freelancers and gig workers. Scan receipts and bank-statement screenshots, file every expense under the right tax category for your country, and keep it all on your phone. No account, no bank linking, no servers holding your data.

Get help

Email punith.nagaraju@cdandlc.com. Please say which phone and Android version you use, and what you were doing when the problem happened. Do not send receipts or ledger contents unless we ask; we can usually help without them.

Your data

Everything you enter stays on your phone. To remove it, use Settings → Your data → Delete all data or uninstall the app. There is no account to delete and no copy on our side.

To keep a copy, use Settings → Your data → Back up to a file.

Launch offer

The first 500 users get every paid feature free with a launch code, redeemed in Google Play (Menu → Pay & subscriptions → Redeem code) or inside the app under Settings → Redeem a launch code.

Important

LedgerLens is a bookkeeping tool, not a tax adviser. Categories, deductible amounts and savings figures are estimates. Confirm your return with your tax authority's guidance or a qualified professional.
