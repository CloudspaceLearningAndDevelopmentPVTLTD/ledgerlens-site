Privacy policy

LedgerLens · Effective 6 October 2026

The short version

LedgerLens keeps your books on your phone. It has no user accounts, no LedgerLens servers, no advertising and no analytics or tracking code. Cloudspace Learning and Development Private Limited cannot see your ledger, your receipts or your questions, because they never leave your device unless you send them somewhere yourself.

Information you enter

Business names, transactions, amounts, categories, notes, receipt and statement images, and your settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are not transmitted to Cloudspace Learning and Development Private Limited or to anyone else by the app.

Reading receipts and statements

Images are read by text recognition that runs on your device (Google ML Kit). The images and the text found in them are not uploaded. Google's ML Kit component may send Google technical information about how the component performs, such as device model, app version, error codes and performance metrics, together with identifiers that are specific to this installation of the app. It does not send your images or their contents. Google describes this in the ML Kit terms and privacy documentation.

On-device AI

If you turn on AI features, a language model runs on your phone: either the model built into the operating system (Gemini Nano on Android, Apple Intelligence on iOS) or an open model you choose to download. Your receipts, amounts and questions are processed on the device and are not sent to a server. Downloading a model fetches a file from huggingface.co; like any download, that site sees your IP address. AI output can be wrong and is not tax advice.

Voice questions and spoken answers

The microphone is used only while you tap it to ask a question by voice. Speech is recognised on your device. If your phone has no on-device speech recognition, voice input stays off unless you choose to allow the phone's own speech service; your voice is then processed by that service (on most Android phones, Google) under its privacy policy. You can withdraw that choice in Settings. LedgerLens does not record or keep audio. Answers are read aloud by the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone.

Exchange rates

To convert foreign-currency amounts the app downloads a public table of daily exchange rates from frankfurter.dev and, as a fallback, from cdn.jsdelivr.net. The request contains only a date. No amount, merchant or identifier is included. As with any internet request, those services see your IP address.

Purchases

Subscriptions, one-time purchases and launch codes are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. Cloudspace Learning and Development Private Limited does not receive your payment card details. The app asks the store which LedgerLens products your store account owns, to unlock them.

Camera, photos and device lock

The camera and your photo library are used only when you choose to scan or import an image, and only the images you select are read. If you turn on App lock, your fingerprint, face or PIN is checked by the operating system; the app only learns whether the check succeeded.

Backups and exports

If device backup is enabled on your phone, the operating system may include LedgerLens data in the encrypted backup kept in your own Google or Apple account. Backup files and tax packages you create in the app go only where you send them, for example to your email or cloud drive.

Reporting an AI answer

When you report an AI answer, the app prepares an email to Cloudspace Learning and Development Private Limited containing the question and the answer. Nothing is sent unless you send that email, and you can edit it first.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. "Delete all data" in Settings erases every business, transaction and receipt image. Uninstalling the app removes them as well. Because there is no server copy, deleted data cannot be recovered by Cloudspace Learning and Development Private Limited.

Security

Data is protected by the operating system's app sandbox and device encryption. You can add App lock and hide the app from screenshots and the recent-apps screen in Settings. Network requests use HTTPS.

Children

LedgerLens is a bookkeeping tool for self-employed adults. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA and India's DPDP Act give you rights over personal data that an organisation holds about you. Cloudspace Learning and Development Private Limited holds none of your LedgerLens data, so you exercise those rights directly on your device: you can view, export, correct and delete everything in the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be included in an app update and published online. Questions: hello@qubit16.ai.
